Little questions, answered.

Can I use it without paying?

Yes. Mouse, six timer durations, chewing sounds, and the Bouncy Marimba alarm are free. There are no ads or subscriptions. Premium is optional.

Does it work when I leave the app?

The countdown keeps track of elapsed time. Allow notifications to receive a completion alert while the app is in the background. Alerts and sounds depend on your device’s notification settings.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up, no login. Just open the app and choose a time. See the privacy policy below for more details.
